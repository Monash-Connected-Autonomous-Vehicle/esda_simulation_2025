Add adaptive-threshold lane detector and WSL setup guide

lane_detection_esda.py subclasses LaneDetectionNode and overrides only
detect_white_lanes, replacing the fixed grayscale threshold of 130 with a
local one plus a guard rejecting masks over a sixth of the ROI.

Over 395 benchmark frames the fixed level drops from 0.800 to 0.678 recall
under a shadow band and from 0.452 to 0.178 precision under a gradient; the
local threshold holds 0.791-0.795 across all four lightings, at 0.9-2.4 ms
per frame against 8.4 ms for the segmentation network.

ui_launch.py gains an Adaptive dropdown option, routes the side cameras to
whichever classic detector the main one uses, and gets the shebang it was
missing (ros2 run could never exec it).

research/ carries the evaluation code behind these numbers.
